Operational knowledge = “scar tissue”: unspecifiable know‑how earned by actually operating in a coupled, shifting system, where each surprise changes both the system and what you know about it. It’s divergent knowledge that others can’t reach just by studying current data or code. Example: you could copy all of Stripe’s current rules and look equivalent on day one. Stripe’s advantage is not today’s rule set; it’s the entangled, path‑dependent understanding of how those rules break under real conditions and how to pre‑empt or contain the damage.
