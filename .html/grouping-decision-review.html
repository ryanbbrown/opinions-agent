candidate-0095 target evidence items

As code and convergent learning become cheap, durable moats come from operational “scar tissue”: path-dependent knowledge and trust earned in coupled, shifting systems. Copying Stripe's current rules cannot reproduce knowing how a Brazilian change cascades through merchants, settlements, and proven rerouting responses. A moat compounds when a well-funded newcomer cannot reach parity by studying today's state; customers pay incumbents to keep learning at reality's pace. But the scar tissue becomes worthless if technology replaces the underlying system rather than changing it.
